Jaideep & Reena – A Vibrant Celebration in Rohtak

Jaideep & Reena's wedding at Sukhvilas Chandigarh was an opulent celebration of royal Punjabi heritage, vibrant joy, and grand architectural beauty. Set among sunken courtyards and lush gardens, every ritual brimmed with energy.

Our team documented the exhilarating dhol rhythms, sun-drenched Haldi moments, and breathtaking royal portraits under the star-lit pavilions.

"Sukhvilas was majestic, but Singh Photos' team preserved every emotion so vividly. Looking through our album brings back all the magic." — Jaideep & Reena
