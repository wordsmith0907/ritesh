THE SINGH STORY

For over four decades, Singh Photos has believed that the most meaningful photographs are found beyond the obvious. In the quiet glances, unguarded laughter, fleeting gestures, and emotions that unfold naturally between people.

As a second-generation photography and cinematography house, we bring together the richness of experience with a contemporary eye — observing rather than interrupting, and documenting each celebration with honesty, artistry, and intention.

From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, we create photographs and films that feel true to the people in them — preserving not just how a moment looked, but how it felt.

Because when the moment passes, the photograph remains.
